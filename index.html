Moonlight smart display

Moonlight is a personal smart display that runs on a Raspberry Pi in the owner's home. It is not a public service and has no user accounts.

What it does

  • Shows the time, the weather, calendars and photos on a screen at home.
  • When the owner's Google Nest doorbell rings, shows a full-screen alert with the camera picture, so the owner can see who is at the door.
  • Can show alerts from the owner's Nest cameras and the reading of the owner's Nest thermostat.

How it uses Google

Moonlight signs in to the owner's own Google account through Google's Device Access programme to read the owner's Nest devices and their events. Everything it receives is used only to draw the owner's display, and is processed on the owner's own Raspberry Pi. Details are in the privacy policy.

Privacy policy · Moonlight is open-source software run privately by its owner.
